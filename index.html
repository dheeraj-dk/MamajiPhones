How to read the comparison

  • Actual RAM matters: 8GB + 8GB extended RAM is an 8GB physical-RAM phone. Virtual RAM uses storage and is not equivalent.
  • Storage speed matters too: UFS 3.1 is generally faster than UFS 2.2. A larger RAM number alone does not make a weaker processor faster.
  • Display quality: a 144Hz HD+ LCD is not automatically better than a 120Hz 1.5K OLED. Sharpness, contrast and real brightness matter.
  • Brightness: local HDR peak brightness is not sustained full-screen outdoor brightness. Manufacturer peak numbers are not directly comparable.
  • OIS: optical image stabilisation helps reduce camera shake. Megapixel counts alone do not establish image quality.
  • Battery: capacity is not a measured runtime. Charging wattage is a maximum, not a constant speed or a full-charge time.
  • Support: update commitments start at launch, not purchase. Battery-health and "fluency" claims are not Android-update promises.
  • Durability: IP ratings do not mean permanent waterproofing. Liquid damage may not be covered by warranty.

  3. Edge seller data: Amazon technical fields were inconsistent. Motorola confirms that an Indian 12GB/256GB configuration exists; check the seller, model and invoice.

  5. Earlier prices: Vivo's Rs. 22,999 was a launch price, not the observed Rs. 34,249 listing price. Use the price snapshot, not launch pricing, to judge this deal.
  6. Fees and offers: Flipkart showed Rs. 199 on most listings and Rs. 129 on P4R. A "Buy at" figure may include conditional offers/cashback and is not an assured upfront discount.
  7. Stock: P4 Pro Birch Wood showed an out-of-stock indication. Verify delivery availability for your PIN code.
  8. Before paying: confirm 256GB, physical RAM, Indian warranty, charger contents, return/service policy, seller and final payable total.
